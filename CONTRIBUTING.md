# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing to Beeswax! ♡

It's meant to stay small, so contributions are made through issues. Pull requests are off.

## Reporting a bug

Open an [issue](https://github.com/flourish-today/beeswax/issues) with:

-   Your phone model and GrapheneOS version
-   What you did, what you expected, and what happened

For security-related issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of a public GitHub issue.

## Verifying a build

If you build a release yourself by following [Reproducible builds](PRIVACY.md#reproducible-builds), please share the result in an issue no matter what you got.

## Suggesting a change

-   Beeswax likely won't add permissions, network access, or libraries.
-   New features are unlikely to be accepted unless they fit what Beeswax already does.
-   These are just guidelines. Don’t worry about it, just reach out.
